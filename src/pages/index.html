What this network does

Edge delivery

Connections terminate at the location nearest to the client, which keeps round trips short and predictable.

Transit & routing

Every location is provisioned with more than one upstream, so traffic can move away from a degraded path.

Operations

Locations are built from a single configuration baseline and watched continuously for capacity and reachability.
